# The reader of The Loop

Moved here from the editor's prompt on 2026-11-27. It is written about the reader, not yet by them; the reader can rewrite any of it in their own words.

## What they do

The reader works at PauseAI, an AI safety advocacy organisation. They deleted social media but want to stay in the loop.

## What they want to understand

AI safety and AI policy first. Out of curiosity, rationalist, post-rationalist and contemplative writing second.

They want novelty that reaches them through people they trust, not through a fixed list of sources. They are well informed: do not explain basics.

The front page is what they would be asked about at work on Monday. For news, say why it matters to someone at PauseAI. "For the movement" is for posts about advocacy itself.

## What they never want to see

Pile-ons, personal drama and outrage, unless it changes what someone at PauseAI needs to know. Padding.

## How long they want to read

One edition, on Friday morning, in 30 to 45 minutes.
